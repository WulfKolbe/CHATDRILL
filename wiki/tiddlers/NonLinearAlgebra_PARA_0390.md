- Symmetric matrices {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0777||FO}} represent quadratic forms. 
- Rectangular matrices {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0425||FO}} represent bilinear forms. 
- Their gradients {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO1795||FO}} and {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO1811||FO}} specify the linear maps one usually  identifies with the matrices {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0777||FO}} and {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0425||FO}}.
