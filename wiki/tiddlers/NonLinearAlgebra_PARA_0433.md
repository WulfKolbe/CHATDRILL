## 10.1. Irreducible Representations

 

The most important groups we study in this chapter are: 
- GL {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO2004||FO}} - the group of linear isomorphisms of a  finite-dimensional vector space {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0317||FO}}. This group has the structure of  an algebraic variety, given by Exercise 8 in Chapter 2. 
- {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO2005||FO}} - the group of volume- and orientation preserving linear automorphisms of {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0317||FO}}, with the structure of an  algebraic variety given by the equation {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO2006||FO}}; 
- {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO1422||FO}} - the group of permutations of a set with {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0015||FO}} elements; this is an  algebraic variety consisting of {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0753||FO}} distinct points in {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO2007||FO}}, namely  the {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0510||FO}} permutation matrices.
