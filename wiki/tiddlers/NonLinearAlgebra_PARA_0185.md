

This quartic is the {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0918||FO}} hyperdeterminant. It vanishes whenever the sur face {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0924||FO}} fails to be smooth in {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0894||FO}}. Hyperdeterminants exist for tensors of  many larger formats. Their study is a fascinating topic in nonlinear algebra.  A standard reference is the book by Gel'fand, Kapranov and Zelevinsky [24]
