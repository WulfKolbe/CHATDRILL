

In many applications one is interested in solving polynomial equations  over the real numbers, and one cares less about non-real complex solutions.  This raises the following important question: Does there exist an analog  of Hilbert's Nullstellensatz over an ordered field, such as the real numbers  {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0003||FO}} ? We shall see that the answer is affirmative. In the next section we  discuss the real Nullstellensatz and the Positivstellensatz. These concern  systems of polynomial equations and inequalities over the real numbers.  They generalize Linear Programming Duality, for systems of linear equations  and linear inequalities over {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0005||FO}}. Moreover, as we shall see in Chapter 12, the  Positivstellensatz plays an important role in Nonlinear Optimization.
