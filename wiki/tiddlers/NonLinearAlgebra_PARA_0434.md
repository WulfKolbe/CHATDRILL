

The groups that we consider have two structures: of an abstract group and  of an algebraic variety. We note that basic group operations, like inverse  or group action, are in fact morphisms of algebraic varieties. We call such  groups algebraic. In what follows, we restrict our attention to algebraic  groups and morphisms between them that are both group morphisms and  morphisms of algebraic varieties. We work over an algebraically closed field  {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0001||FO}} of characteristic zero. 

In general, the following strategy to study an object can be very power ful: 
- consider all maps from (resp. to) this object into (resp. from) an other basic object.
