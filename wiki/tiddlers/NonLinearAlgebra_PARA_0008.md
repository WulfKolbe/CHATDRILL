# Chapter 1

 

\title{
Polynomial Rings
} 
"Algebra is but written geometry", Sophie Germain 

A natural next step after linear algebra is commutative algebra. In that  subject area one studies algebraic structures such as fields, rings and ideals.  In this first chapter we introduce the relevant basics, with a focus on poly nomials and Gröbner basis. We show how to use these for computing basic  invariants of a polynomial ideal, like dimension or degree. The formalism  we develop now will be applied to geometric situations in later chapters.
