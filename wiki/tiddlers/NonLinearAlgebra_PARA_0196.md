## 4.3. The Image of a Polynomial Map

 

We discussed methods for computing the Zariski closure of the image of a  polynomial map. Can we say something about the image itself? The answer  is 'yes', but the situation very much depends on the field {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0001||FO}} and whether we  are in the projective case or the affine case. In this section we discuss tools  for computing such images. We begin by highlighting the difference between  the real numbers and complex numbers with regard to this problem.
