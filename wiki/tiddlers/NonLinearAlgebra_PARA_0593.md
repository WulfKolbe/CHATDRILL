

This is almost a monomial map! Indeed, the only thing that changes are  the constants given by minors of the matrix {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO2599||FO}}. However, these constants  do not depend on {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO2606||FO}} and hence we may define an automorphism of  {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO2594||FO}} that turns the orbit to an image of a monomial map, by simply  rescaling the coordinates. 

At this point one could have a false impression that the orbit is isomor phic to the image of a monomial map defined by all squarefree monomials  of degree {{NonLinearAlgebra_FO0592||FO}}. This is not the case, as some of the monomials may not appear  at all. This happens if the corresponding minor was equal to zero - then we  cannot rescale it.
