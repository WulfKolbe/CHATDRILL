## 5.3. Schubert Calculus

 

In this section we show how Grassmannians can help us answer enumera tive questions. We would like to know how many lines or planes in space  satisfy some properties. This general subject area is known as enumera tive geometry, and the specific answers we provide are based on Schubert  calculus. Thus, we introduce an intersection theory for subvarieties of a  Grassmannian.
